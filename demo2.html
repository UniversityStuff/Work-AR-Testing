Demo 2 · A whole site on the table

Put the four printed corner sheets into the four corners of a table. Each sheet says which corner it belongs to; the arrows point towards the side you stand on.

  • The first time, step back until at least three sheets are in view. The demo measures the table by itself.
  • Then the model fills the whole table. Walk around it.
  • Drag, pinch and twist to move, zoom and turn the model; ⤢ Reset view puts it back.
  • 📏 re-measures or lets you type the table size.
