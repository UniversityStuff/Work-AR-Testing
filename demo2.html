Demo 2 · Four stations, one job

Tape the four printed station sheets (A–D) at different spots in the room, a few meters apart. The job sends you between them in a fixed order.

  • Walk to the station shown on the card and point the camera at its sheet.
  • At the test bench you have to keep the camera on the station while it calibrates.
  • Every station shows live info when you look at it.
